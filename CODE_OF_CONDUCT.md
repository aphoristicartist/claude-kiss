# Code of conduct

Claude KISS welcomes technically rigorous disagreement and expects people to remain
respectful while having it.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Assume good faith while respecting clearly stated boundaries.
- Make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Protect private information and remove sensitive data from reports and examples.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is
representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to keep those spaces
safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Report a vulnerability** form
in the Security tab and identify the report as a conduct matter. The maintainer will review
the report privately, avoid conflicts of interest where possible, and share only the
information needed to investigate and respond.
